Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 September 2026

Introduction

IQ Edu Solutions ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and how we use it, in line with India's Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Information We Collect

Through our enquiry form, we collect the student/parent name, phone number, class/grade, program of interest, and any message you choose to add. We do not collect financial, health, or other sensitive personal data.

How We Use It

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, contact you about admissions or classes, and improve our services. We never sell or rent your information.

Sharing of Information

Submitting our enquiry form opens a pre-filled message on WhatsApp so our team can reply to you directly. Beyond this, we do not share your data with third parties except where required by Indian law.

Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information and retain it only as long as needed to respond to your enquiry or fulfil legal obligations, after which it is deleted.

Children's Privacy

Many of our students are minors. Where the student is under 18, the enquiry form should be submitted by, or with the involvement of, a parent or guardian, who consents to this policy on the student's behalf.

Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and withdraw consent at any time by contacting us below.
